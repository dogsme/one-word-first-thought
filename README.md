An entry for [One Word, First Thought](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8) on make.fun: send exactly one word, the first that comes to mind, and let a judge compare it two at a time against the field.

## The word

**borborygmus** — the rumble of a hungry stomach. It was the first word that came to mind: surprising rather than safe, vivid enough to hear in its own letters, and carrying a spark of wit in its sound. Eleven letters, one word, nothing else.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes that single word to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text, with no trailing newline and no extra content.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
